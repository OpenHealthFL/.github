## About Us

**OpenHealthFL** is an open research and engineering initiative dedicated to advancing privacy-preserving artificial intelligence in healthcare.

We explore how healthcare institutions can collaboratively develop and evaluate AI models without requiring the centralization of sensitive patient data.

Our work focuses on the technical, scientific and infrastructural challenges of deploying federated intelligence in resource-constrained healthcare environments, particularly across Africa.

We believe that access to collaborative medical intelligence should not depend on the size of a hospital, the quality of its network infrastructure or its geographical location.

## Our Mission

To design, develop and evaluate open, secure and resource-efficient federated AI architectures that support collaborative clinical intelligence while respecting institutional data governance and patient privacy.

| Research Area | Focus |

## Our Approach

We work toward a healthcare AI ecosystem in which:

- Patient datasets remain under the control of their respective healthcare institutions.
- Hospitals collaborate through federated model training.
- Data quality and institutional heterogeneity are considered during model development.
- Privacy-enhancing mechanisms are integrated into the learning process.
- Communication-efficient architectures accommodate unreliable connectivity.
- Research findings are reproducible, transparent and scientifically evaluated.

## Featured Research Initiative

### OpenHealthFL Framework

Our flagship initiative investigates a modular federated learning architecture for privacy-preserving clinical decision support.

**Core research directions:**
- Quality-aware federated aggregation.
- Differential privacy and privacy–utility evaluation.
- IID and non-IID federated learning benchmarks.
- Communication-efficient model updates.
- Clinical data harmonization using HL7 FHIR.
- Distributed healthcare AI in low-resource environments.

**Development period:** 2026–2028.

[Explore the OpenHealthFL repositories](https://github.com/OpenHealthFL)
